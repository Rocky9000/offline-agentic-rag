# Maintenance Schedule

Maintenance is tracked by flight hours in the fleet tracker.

## Every 25 flight hours
Replace all propellers, inspect motor bearings for noise, and re-torque frame screws.

## Every 100 flight hours
Replace motors that fail the vibration test, recalibrate the IMU and compass, and update the flight controller firmware to the version approved by the avionics team.

## Unscheduled maintenance
Any hard landing or crash grounds the aircraft until a technician signs off a full inspection. Grounded aircraft get a red tag on the airframe.
