# Incident Reporting

An incident is any crash, flyaway, injury, property damage, or near miss with a manned aircraft.

## What to do
Make the area safe first. Then notify the field lead by phone within 1 hour. File a written report in the incident portal within 24 hours, including photos, the flight log file, and weather conditions.

## Flyaways
If an aircraft flies away, record the last known GPS position and direction of travel immediately. Call the local air traffic control unit if the aircraft could enter controlled airspace.

## Investigation
The safety officer reviews every report within 5 working days. Reports are blameless: the goal is to fix causes, not to punish people.
