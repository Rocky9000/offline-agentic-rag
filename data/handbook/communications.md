# Field Communications

## Radios
Crews use handheld radios on channel 4 for routine coordination and channel 9 for emergencies only. Do a radio check before the first flight of the day.

## Network
The field laptops connect to the office through the company VPN. Public Wi-Fi may be used only with the VPN switched on.

## Daily status
The field lead posts a short status update in the operations channel by 6 pm on every site day, covering flights completed, issues, and the plan for the next day.
