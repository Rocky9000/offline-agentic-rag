# Travel and Reimbursement

## Booking
Book trains and flights through the travel desk at least 7 days before departure. Economy class is the default for trips under 6 hours.

## Daily allowance
Field staff receive a daily allowance of 1,500 rupees for meals on site days. Hotel stays are reimbursed up to 4,000 rupees per night in metro cities and 2,500 rupees per night elsewhere.

## Claims
Submit expense claims in the finance portal within 15 days of returning, with itemised receipts. Claims without receipts are not reimbursed. Approved claims are paid with the next monthly salary.
