# Battery Care

All field drones at Kestrel Field Robotics use 6S lithium-polymer packs rated at 22.2 volts.

## Charging
Charge packs only on the balance chargers in the battery room, never on vehicle chargers. The standard charge rate is 1C. Fast charging at 2C is allowed only when the field lead approves it in the log book. Never leave a charging pack unattended.

## Storage
Packs that will not be flown within 48 hours must be brought to storage voltage of 3.8 volts per cell. Store them in the fireproof cabinet at room temperature.

## Retirement
Retire any pack that is puffed, has a dented case, or shows more than 20 percent capacity loss on the monthly capacity test. Retired packs go in the orange disposal bin and are collected by the recycling vendor every Friday.
