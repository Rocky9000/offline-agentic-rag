# Onboarding for New Field Engineers

## First week
New field engineers spend the first week shadowing a senior pilot. They complete the battery handling course and the incident reporting course in the learning portal.

## Simulator hours
Before flying a company aircraft, a new engineer must log 10 hours in the flight simulator and pass a practical check flight with the chief pilot.

## Equipment issued
Each engineer receives a high-visibility vest, a hard hat, a radio, and a field laptop. Lost equipment must be reported to the stores manager within 2 days.
