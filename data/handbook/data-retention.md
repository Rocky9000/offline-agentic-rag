# Data Retention Policy

Survey imagery and telemetry are customer data and are handled under this policy.

## Retention periods
Raw imagery is kept for 90 days after project delivery and then deleted. Processed deliverables such as orthomosaics and 3D models are kept for 2 years. Flight logs are kept for 5 years because regulators can request them.

## Storage and access
All customer data is stored encrypted on the internal file server. Copying customer data to personal devices or personal cloud accounts is prohibited. Access is granted per project by the data steward.

## Deletion requests
If a customer asks for early deletion, the data steward must complete it within 30 days and send written confirmation.
