# Flight Safety Rules

Every flight needs a pilot in command and a visual observer. The pilot in command must hold a current remote pilot certificate.

## Weather limits
Do not fly when sustained wind exceeds 10 metres per second or gusts exceed 14 metres per second. Do not fly in rain, fog, or when visibility is below 3 kilometres.

## Altitude and distance
The maximum altitude is 120 metres above ground level. Keep the aircraft within visual line of sight at all times. Stay at least 30 metres away from people who are not part of the crew.

## Pre-flight checklist
Before every flight, check propellers for cracks, confirm GPS lock with at least 12 satellites, verify the return-to-home altitude, and confirm the geofence is loaded. Write the checklist result in the flight log.
